Digital service notice

Incognito AI Pro is a digital software subscription. No physical item is shipped.

Cancellation

Users may contact support to request cancellation of future billing or to report an issue related to an active subscription. Cancellation requests do not automatically reverse a completed payment for a subscription term that has already started.

Refunds

Refund requests are reviewed case by case. Refunds may be considered in situations such as duplicate payment, accidental multiple charges, or technical issues that prevent subscription activation and cannot be reasonably resolved.

Non-refundable situations

  • Refunds may be declined if the subscription has already been successfully activated and substantially consumed.
  • Refunds may be declined for issues outside the product's control, including third-party service outages or unsupported system environments.
  • Promotional or discounted purchases may be subject to separate limitations where clearly disclosed at the time of purchase.

How to request a refund

To request a refund or report a billing issue, email dev.linuxto5re@gmail.com with the payment reference, purchase date, and a short description of the issue.

Resolution

Approved refunds, if any, are processed back through the original payment method according to the payment gateway and banking timelines.
