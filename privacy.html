Overview

  • Configuration data such as selected settings, preferences, and subscription details.

How information is used

  • To improve product stability, security, and user experience.

Payment processing

Payments are processed through third-party payment infrastructure. Users may be redirected to a secure checkout flow managed by the payment provider. Payment gateway partners may collect and process information according to their own privacy practices and regulatory obligations.

Data sharing

Information may be retained for as long as needed for account management, subscription support, compliance, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legitimate business operations.
